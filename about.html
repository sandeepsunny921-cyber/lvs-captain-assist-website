About LVS Tech Solutions

The story, mission, and regulatory adherence metrics behind LVS Captain Assist Pro.

Who We Are

LVS Tech Solutions is a forward-thinking Indian software and operational service provider. Our flagship solution, LVS Captain Assist Pro, is designed specifically for commercial drivers, delivery professionals, and transport operators (generically known as "Captains") who form the backbone of urban transport and logistics across the country.

Our service provides advanced fuel efficiency route mapping, automatic trip and billing ledger compilation, and roadside helpdesk integrations. By subscribing to LVS Captain Assist Pro, our members gain tools that dramatically lower their operation costs, increase billing transparency, and protect their physical safety.

Our Core Objectives

At LVS Tech Solutions, our operations are founded on three main principles:

01. Economy

Reducing everyday operational expenditure (fuel, route loss) through high-quality telemetry and mapping aids.

02. Safety

Providing quick links to registered emergency mechanics and roadside guide listings during midnight operations.

03. Compliance

Supporting easy tax accounting and legitimate business billing for solo contractors via smart receipt builders.

Secured Financial Architecture

As an official partner integrating Razorpay secure APIs, LVS Tech Solutions takes financial compliance with absolute seriousness. All billing statements, invoice templates, and payment schedules are governed strictly under governing consumer welfare acts.

We never store sensitive card details or personal identification metrics on our servers. Transactions are fully routed through Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant interface to guarantee the highest safety levels for our captains and enterprise fleet managers.
