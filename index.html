Help & Support

Frequently Answered Queries

Important billing and operational questions for our users.

How do subscription payments work?

We process subscription renewals monthly. Payments are fully handled using Razorpay's secure SDK. You will receive an automated email receipt immediately upon transaction execution.

What is the Refund & Cancellation Policy?

You can cancel your subscription at any time. There are no penalty charges. Refunds are processed if double-billing errors occur on our end, or if a service issue remains unresolved. Refunds are processed through Razorpay back to your original source instrument (Card, NetBanking, UPI) within 5 to 7 business days. See our full policy in our Terms.

How can I reach support regarding a payment?

For any payment anomalies, receipt disputes, or subscription cancellations, please reach out to our team at support@lvscaptainassist.com or call us directly. All tickets receive formal replies within 24 operational hours.
