Privacy Policy Statement

Effective Date: July 8, 2026. This policy outlines how LVS Tech Solutions processes, retains, and secures user data.

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to LVS Captain Assist Pro, operated by LVS Tech Solutions ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to safeguarding the confidentiality and integrity of personal information shared by our driver-partners, delivery captains, and client fleet managers. This Privacy Policy outlines our procedures concerning data collection, transmission, cloud storage, and security frameworks.

By registering, purchasing premium subscription packages, or utilizing the LVS Captain Assist Pro dashboard, you explicitly assent to the handling of your data in accordance with the terms declared herein.

2. Category of Information We Collect

To activate and optimize the full potential of our assistance tools, we compile the following variables:

  • Identification Records: Full legal name, contact telephone digit, billing address, profile avatar, and driver license metrics (for validation).
  • Configuration & Automation Settings: Customized pricing-per-km targets, ride filter parameters, auto-accept speed settings, and interactive popup specifications.
  • Device Specifications: IP numbers, operating firmware variants, unique device identifiers, and logging telemetry metrics.
  • Transaction Histories: Transaction ID coordinates, subscribed packages, billing dates, and invoice tracking data. We do NOT store card raw numbers or CVVs. All checkout cycles are processed via secure iframe tokens directly by Razorpay.

3. Utilization of Your Variables

Your details are applied strictly for the following operational workflows:

  • Establishing, maintaining, and authenticating user subscription parameters.
  • Providing accurate, sub-second Ride Alert Detection and floating Overlay Popups.
  • Enabling secure daily payment billing protocols via our Razorpay integration.
  • Syncing custom automation settings, driver parameter thresholds, and ride histories securely via Firebase Sync.
  • Compiling detailed ride history archives and automated subscription receipts.

4. Security Measures & Payment Integrations

LVS Tech Solutions maintains bank-grade data security strategies to safeguard private identifiers from unauthorized access. Since our monetization pipeline utilizes Razorpay secure merchant services, checkout procedures comply with the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI-DSS).

All critical credit credentials, bank authorizations, and UPI keys are collected via secure web tokens by Razorpay, meaning no financial credentials ever touch or reside inside LVS Tech Solutions' databases.

5. Information Disclosures to Third Parties

We enforce strict rules against selling, licensing, or renting user personal databases to marketing agencies. Information is shared strictly with:

  • Payment Services: Razorpay, to execute payment intents and premium renewal bills.
  • Database Cloud Services: Secure cloud hosting providers, exclusively to run the encrypted real-time Firebase database synchronization.

6. Your Rights & Data Custody

Under prevailing national communication laws, you hold the legal right to review, update, or purge your collected credentials. You can opt to stop GPS logging at any time by turning off location permissions in your app settings, although this will limit route calculation features.

To submit an official request for database deletion or data summary extracts, please write to our dedicated compliance desk at support@lvscaptainassist.com.

7. Revisions to This Statement

LVS Tech Solutions reserves the authority to modify this Privacy Policy whenever operational updates or national compliance regulations alter. Revisions are effective instantly upon website publication. We encourage users to inspect this directory periodically.

Need Clarification on Data Security?

Our data protection officer is available for all privacy and Razorpay payment safety-related inquiries.
